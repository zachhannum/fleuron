## The Inventory

These gentlemen, having pen, ink, and paper, about them, made an exact inventory of every thing they saw; and when they had done, desired I would set them down, that they might deliver it to the emperor.

| Where it was found | What the searchers took it for | What it was |
|:---|:---|---:|
| The right coat-pocket | A foot-cloth for his majesty's chief room of state | A handkerchief |
| The left coat-pocket | A huge silver chest, full of a sort of dust | A snuff-box |
| The right waistcoat-pocket | A bundle of white thin substances, marked with black figures | A journal-book |
| The left waistcoat-pocket | An engine with twenty long poles | A comb |
| The large pocket of the middle cover | A hollow pillar of iron, fastened to a strong piece of timber | A pocket pistol |
| The smaller right pocket | Round flat pieces of white and red metal | Silver and copper money |
| The smaller left pocket | Two black pillars irregularly shaped | A razor and a knife |
| The right fob | A globe, half silver, that makes an incessant noise | A watch |
| The left fob | A net almost large enough for a fisherman | A purse |
| The girdle, on the left side | A sword of the length of five men | A scimitar |
| The girdle, on the right side | A pouch divided into two cells | A pouch of powder and bullets |

My scimitar, pistols, and pouch, were conveyed in carriages to his majesty's stores; but the rest of my goods were returned me.
